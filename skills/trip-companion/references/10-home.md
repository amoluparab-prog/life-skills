# Phase 10: Coming home

Purpose: an easy return, and one small lesson kept for next time.

## Steps
1. **Return check-in:** look up when it opens for the return airline and do phase 6 again. Reconfirm the flight time; schedules change during a trip.
2. **Security on the way back:** the rules of the country they are leaving apply now, and may differ from the outbound ones (liquids, batteries, duty-free on connections). Look them up as in phase 5.
3. **Leave time:** work it back as in phase 7, with hotel check-out time, returning a hire car, and getting to an airport they know less well.
4. **What to declare at home:** the home country's customs allowances for goods, alcohol, tobacco and cash, and its rules on food, plants and animal products. Look up on the home customs page and cite it. Keep receipts for larger purchases handy.
5. **Before leaving the stay:** safe, sockets, bathroom, under the beds; passports counted; a tax-refund form stamped at the airport if they are claiming one.
6. **After landing:** how they get home, and anything to claim (delay, lost bag, insurance) with the deadline for claiming.
7. **Next time note.** Ask two questions and write the answers in three lines at the end of the trip sheet: what worked that they would do again, and what they would do differently (packed too much, connection too tight, wrong area to stay). Offer to keep it for their next trip if the session can save files.

## Done when
They are home, claims are filed or noted with deadlines, and the next-time note is written.

## Dated items to offer
Return check-in opening, check-out time, leave-for-airport time, claim deadlines, and passport expiry if it falls within the next year.
