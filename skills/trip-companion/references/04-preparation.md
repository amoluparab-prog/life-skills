# Phase 4: Preparation timeline

Purpose: spread the jobs out so nothing is left for the last night. Build the list from this trip; delete what does not apply, and for a short domestic trip fold it all into one short list.

If the trip is closer than a bucket, move its items into the next one and put the urgent ones first. If the traveller is looking ahead from months away, give the list and say that anything looked up now (rules, check-in times) must be checked again nearer the date.

## A month out (or as soon as the trip is fixed)
- Documents: passports valid for as long as the destination requires (look up; never ask for the number); visas or travel authorisations applied for; consent letter if a child travels with one parent; for anyone who is not a citizen of the country they live in, the permit or visa they need to get back in, valid on the return date (look up on that country's immigration page); driving permit if hiring a car.
- Health: vaccinations or a travel clinic visit if the health source says so; enough prescription medication for the trip plus spare, a copy of the prescription, and a check that each medicine is legal at the destination.
- Insurance: travel insurance bought as soon as the first booking is paid for, covering medical care, cancellation, any existing medical condition and what they plan to do; check whether existing health cover or a card already covers them.
- Assistance: request airport or airline assistance, special meals, bassinets early.
- Money: check card expiry dates, foreign transaction fees, and whether the bank needs to be told.
- Big bookings that sell out: airport transfers, car hire, key tickets.

## A week out
- Reconfirm flights and stays; check for schedule changes.
- Phone and data: roaming plan, local SIM or eSIM; download offline maps and the airline's app.
- Money: a small amount of local cash if useful; a second card kept apart from the first.
- Copies: bookings, insurance policy and emergency number, and a photo of each passport's photo page saved on the phone and shared with someone at home. The traveller does this themselves; do not ask to see them.
- Home: mail, plants, pets, bins, someone with a key.
- Check the forecast and start the packing list (phase 5).
- Work trip: out-of-office and handover; meeting address and how to get there from the stay on the first morning; the employer's travel and expenses rules, and keeping receipts as they go.
- Domestic flight: check on the official page which ID the airport accepts (for example the security authority's ID page) and that theirs is in date.

## Two days out
- Online check-in if open (phase 6); boarding passes saved offline.
- Pack, weigh the bags against the airline's limits.
- Charge everything; pack chargers and adaptors.
- Book or plan transport to the airport; work out the leave time (phase 7).
- Refill medication for the journey into hand luggage.
- Look again at the travel advisory and entry rules for changes.

## The night before
- Documents, wallet, phone, keys and medication laid out in one place.
- Flight status checked; alarm set, with a second one.
- Bags by the door; liquids bag and electronics easy to reach.
- Fridge, bins, windows, heating or cooling set.
- Phone on charge.

## Done when
The traveller has the list with dates on it and knows which items are theirs to do this week.

## Dated items to offer
Each bucket as a dated reminder, plus insurance purchase, visa deadline, and "check in online" at the time it opens.
