# Phase 7: Leaving the house

Purpose: walk out on time with everything, without a last-minute scramble.

## Work out when to leave
Work backwards from the scheduled departure and show the sum:

1. **Be at the airport by:** the airline's and the airport's own advice for this flight, and never later than the bag-drop or check-in deadline. If neither can be found, a common default is about 2 hours before a domestic flight and 3 hours before an international one; say it is a default. Check too when the bag-drop desk opens, so that an early arrival is not spent at a closed desk.
2. **Adjust once, do not stack.** The default already allows for dropping a bag. If any of these apply, add one allowance of 15 to 30 minutes in total, not one each: children or a group, assistance, a busy holiday period, an airport they do not know. Someone with carry-on only who has checked in online needs no extra, and may reasonably cut the domestic default a little; say that it is their call. Add 20 to 30 minutes for a car park with a shuttle bus or for returning a hire car; if they only said "I park at the airport", assume the shorter figure and say so. (These amounts are rules of thumb.)
3. **Travel time to the airport** at that hour on that day: look it up if a maps tool is available, otherwise ask them. Add a cushion of 20 to 30 minutes, more if there is one road or one train.
4. **Getting out of the door:** 15 minutes, more with children.

For a short trip, give the time to be at the airport for the flight back as well, and finish that sum when they say where they will leave from. Sanity-check the result: if it has someone leaving more than about four hours before a domestic flight, look for a stacked allowance.

The leave time is when they pull away from the house; getting out of the door starts before it. Parking or car-return time is added on top of the single allowance in step 2.

Give the result as one line: "Leave home at 06:10. That gets you to the airport at 07:15 for a 10:15 departure." Book the taxi, check the train times or the parking the day before.

## The door checklist
On the day, or the night before, read it out short, in this order, and ask them to tick each. If they are only looking ahead, give the leave time now and keep the checklist for the night before.

- Passports or ID for everyone, and visas or authorisations
- Boarding passes (phone and backup)
- Wallet: cards, some cash
- Phone, and it is charged
- Chargers, power bank (in the carry-on, not the checked bag), adaptor
- Medication, glasses
- Keys, and who has the spare
- Bags counted: how many checked, how many carry-on
- Home: windows and doors locked, cooker and taps off, heating or cooling set, bins out, fridge cleared, plants and pets arranged
- Flight status checked one last time

Add anything specific to this trip (child's comfort toy, work laptop, gift, medical device).

## Done when
They have a leave time with the working shown, and on the day the checklist ticked.

## Dated items to offer
"Leave for the airport" with an alert 30 minutes before, the taxi pickup, the alarm.
