# Phase 6: Check-in and seats

Purpose: checked in early, boarding passes in hand, seats sorted.

## Steps
1. **Look up on the operating airline's site** when online check-in opens and closes for this route (often 24 to 48 hours before departure, but it varies, and some routes need documents checked at the airport). Give the opening time in the traveller's local time. If the airline's page cannot be opened, you may say "often 24 to 48 hours before; not verified, check the airline's app". The same goes for bag-drop and gate cut-offs: when the airline's page will not open, point them to the airline's app or their booking confirmation.
2. **Tell them what they will need to hand:** booking reference and surname, passport details for international flights, any visa or authorisation number. They type these in themselves on the airline's site or app. Do not ask them to share these with you.
3. **Seats.**
   - If a seat-picker skill is installed, hand over to it.
   - Otherwise, simple guidance: aisle for easy movement and long legs, window for sleep and views; forward for a quick exit or a tight connection; over the wing for a steadier ride; avoid the last row and seats by toilets or galleys if they want quiet; exit rows have rules about who may sit there. Families: check the airline's policy on seating children with an adult before paying for seats. Anyone with an assistance need: ask the airline, which may seat them without a fee.
   - Say what a seat costs before recommending a paid one.
4. **Boarding passes.** Save to the phone wallet and as an offline screenshot or PDF; print one copy if the airline or the country requires paper, or if a phone might die. Check the name, date and flight on each.
5. **Bags.** Confirm the bag-drop deadline and whether to pre-pay for bags online, which is usually cheaper than at the airport (look up).
6. **If online check-in fails** (document check needed, infant on the booking, a codeshare flight sold by one airline and flown by another): it is common, not an emergency. Plan to arrive earlier and use the desk.

## Done when
Everyone is checked in with a boarding pass saved, or they know why they must check in at the airport and by when.

## Dated items to offer
"Online check-in opens" (alarm at the opening time), "check-in closes", bag-drop deadline.
