# Phase 9: Arrival and during the trip

Purpose: a smooth first hour, then a light touch for the rest of the trip.

## Before landing (or the day before)
- **Immigration:** what the officer may ask for, looked up for this nationality and country: passport, visa or authorisation, arrival form or app if there is one, address of the first stay, return ticket, proof of funds. Have the first stay's address to hand offline.
- **Customs:** what must be declared (food, plants, medicines, cash above the limit, goods over the allowance), from the destination's customs page. When in doubt, declare.
- **Getting into town:** two options with time, rough cost and how to pay, checked against the arrival time (the last train, late-night choices), and the accessible option if needed. Official taxi ranks or booked transfers over offers in the arrivals hall.
- **Money:** whether cards are widely taken, where to get cash at a fair rate, and to choose the local currency when a card machine asks.
- **Connectivity:** switch on the roaming plan or eSIM, or where to buy a SIM; offline maps as backup.

## On the ground
- **Tell someone at home** they have arrived.
- **Copies of bookings** saved offline and shared with a fellow traveller; emergency numbers for the country, the insurer and the embassy or consulate noted (look them up).
- **A short daily plan** when they ask for it, not a schedule imposed on them: the one main thing, how to get there, opening hours checked that day, one fallback for bad weather, and the forecast. Keep it to a few lines.
- **Re-check dated things** as they come up: the next stay's check-in, a cancellation deadline, the return flight's time.
- **If something goes wrong** (illness, lost passport, theft): local emergency services first, then the insurer's emergency line, then the traveller's embassy or consulate, with the numbers looked up. Safety before plans.

## Done when
They are in their first stay with money and data working, and they know where the trip sheet and the booking copies are.

## Dated items to offer
Each stay change, booked activities, the return flight and its check-in opening.
